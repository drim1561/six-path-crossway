How it works

Six Path Crossway is a fan made helper for MapleStory Classic players. It watches your game window while you train and turns your level into a plan.

Grind

The Grind tab keeps score while you train. Press Share game window, pick MapleStory and go. There's nothing to type: it reads your EXP bar, your level and the potions on your quickslots straight off the screen, so every potion you drink needs a quickslot.

  • Pop out over the game floats the live numbers in a small window over MapleStory.
  • EXP an hour counts from your first kill to your latest, so walking out of town doesn't drag it down.
  • All time counts the Lemons and Meat you've eaten, and Your record keeps your hours, levels and best sessions.
  • Count my loot is optional. Flip through your Equip, Use and Etc tabs at the start and the end, and it tells you if the session was Profitable or Losing in mesos.

Signed in, your sessions save to your account and nobody else can see them. Signed out, they stay on this device. The reading happens on your computer.

Why post

Boss camping comes down to patience and luck, and whoever does the most damage takes the loot. A timer won't change that. What it does change is how long everyone waits. Every kill or spawn you share makes the window sharper for the next person, and for you next time.

Reading the board

Each line is one channel. The bar shows when the boss could spawn, and the red line is now.

  • Due nowThe window is open. It could appear any minute.
  • Opens soonSomeone posted a kill and the window hasn't started yet.
  • WatchedSomeone is camping the channel and hasn't seen it spawn.
  • UpIt just spawned and may still be alive.
  • OverdueThe window passed with no report. Someone probably killed it without posting.

Times are game chat time, with your local time in grey beside them. Click a channel to see who posted what.

Posting

Sign in with Discord, then press a button or type a line and hit Enter.

  • ch35 kill 7:35You killed it on channel 35 at 7:35 game time.
  • ch12 spawnedIt's up on channel 12 right now.
  • ch7 waitingYou're camping channel 7 and it hasn't shown yet.
  • heard ch3 9:10Someone told you it died on channel 3 at 9:10.

The buttons post for the last channel you used. Made a mistake? Click the channel and press Undo, any time in the next 10 minutes.

How the window learns

The game files say 60 minutes for Mushmom, but real camps run longer. Only a kill followed by a spawn that the same person watched counts as a camp, and their Still waiting posts help too. Until a boss has 5 camps the window is a wide guess, from the game timer to twice that. Heard posts show dashed and never count.

Builds

Pick your class and type just your level to get a leveling guide: where to train, where your skill and ability points go, and what to wear and scroll. Skill paths compares two ways to spend your skill points, with the hours each one takes, and the Quests tab lists what to do next, from quests open now to ones coming up.

Tired of typing your stats? Read from game reads them off your screen: share your game window or paste a screenshot, with your Character Stat window (DETAIL on) and Skill window open. It marks every box it filled, and leaves a box empty when it isn't sure. The reading happens on your computer.

Numbers come from the game's own files and meowdb's Classic damage formulas. Claws, one handed swords and polearms have been matched to real stat windows, and other weapons carry an Estimated note until one is. Saving a build needs Discord and shows the character name you type, never your Discord name.

Appraiser

The Appraiser tab answers one question: what will this item sell for? Pick the item and type what its tooltip says: the + number, slots left, and the bonus on each stat. Paste the listings from its Cozy page, or tell us what yours sold for, and it works out a price from the items most like yours and says how sure it is. The Odds tab shows how rare your item is, from the game's own scroll odds, and the Drops tab links to meowdb, where Classic players vote on who drops what.

Prices are what you type. The Check on Cozy button copies the item name and opens Cozy Market, where you paste it into the search. How far a dropped item's stats can swing isn't in the game files, so when a scroll story leaves a point or two over, the page calls it the drop roll.

The answer is a ceiling, not what a buyer pays. When lots of the same item are already for sale, it often sells for much less, so check Cozy's listings with the same stats too.

Good to know

EXP an hour covers your whole run by default. That's the steadiest number, but it moves slowly once you're deep into a run.

Whenever something changes your pace, press 5m or 10m on the EXP an hour switch to see just the last few minutes. Good times to do it: someone joins or leaves your party, you go solo, or you're an hour in.

Fair play

One post every 15 seconds. Your Discord name shows on everything you post. False posts get hidden and the account blocked.
