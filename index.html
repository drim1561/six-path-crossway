How it works

Why post

Boss camping comes down to patience and luck, and whoever does the most damage takes the loot. A timer won't change that. What it does change is how long everyone waits. Every kill or spawn you share makes the window sharper for the next person, and for you next time.

Reading the board

Each line is one channel. The bar shows when the boss could spawn, and the red line is now.

  • Due nowThe window is open. It could appear any minute.
  • Opens soonSomeone posted a kill and the window hasn't started yet.
  • WatchedSomeone is camping the channel and hasn't seen it spawn.
  • UpIt just spawned and may still be alive.
  • OverdueThe window passed with no report. Someone probably killed it without posting.

Times are game chat time, with your local time in grey beside them. Click a channel to see who posted what.

Posting

Sign in with Discord, then press a button or type a line and hit Enter.

  • ch35 kill 7:35You killed it on channel 35 at 7:35 game time.
  • ch12 spawnedIt's up on channel 12 right now.
  • ch7 waitingYou're camping channel 7 and it hasn't shown yet.
  • heard ch3 9:10Someone told you it died on channel 3 at 9:10.

The buttons post for the last channel you used. Made a mistake? Click the channel and press Undo, any time in the next 10 minutes.

How the window learns

The game files say 60 minutes for Mushmom, but real camps run longer. Only a kill followed by a spawn that the same person watched counts as a camp, and their Still waiting posts help too. Until a boss has 5 camps the window is a wide guess, from the game timer to twice that. Heard posts show dashed and never count.

Builds

The Builds tab turns your stat window into a leveling guide: where to train, where your skill and ability points go, and what to wear and scroll. Monster, skill and item numbers come from the game's own files, and the damage math is meowdb's Classic formulas. Where to train is ranked by EXP per cast, from a few thousand simulated kills with your damage range, crits, monster defense and hit chance.

Tired of typing your stats? The Read from game button on the Builds tab reads them off your screen. Press it, share your game window or paste a screenshot, then open your Character Stat window with DETAIL and your Skill window and click through your 1st and 2nd job tabs. It fills in your level, stats, attack or magic, accuracy, crits and skill levels, marks every box it filled so you can check it, and stops by itself once it has everything. If it isn't sure about a number it leaves the box empty and tells you which one to type. The reader never sends your game pictures anywhere, because the reading happens on your computer. Saving a build is separate. It stores the numbers in the form and the name and note you type, and uploads only the equip screenshot you pick yourself.

Pick your 1st job tile, then where you're heading or your 2nd job, and type just your level to get a guide. Every other box makes it sharper. Physical classes also pick their weapon, since damage depends on it. Each weapon type has its own damage multiplier from meowdb's Classic table. Claws, one handed swords and polearms have been matched to real stat windows, and a guide for any other weapon carries an Estimated note until one is. Want yours checked? Send Rimmah a screenshot of your stat window with DETAIL open and say what weapon you're holding.

Saving needs Discord, and a build shows the character name you type, never your Discord name. Screenshots are shown as they are and the numbers are what each player typed, so take other people's builds as their word.

Appraiser

The Appraiser tab answers one question: what would this item cost, on average, to make yourself? Type the + number, slots left and the bonus from its tooltip, and it works out how it was likely scrolled, then counts the clean bases and scrolls it takes to get one that good. Slots, clean stats and scroll odds come from the game's own files.

Prices are what you type. The Check on Cozy button copies the item name and opens Cozy Market, where you paste it into the search. How far a dropped item's stats can swing isn't in the game files, so when a scroll story leaves a point or two over, the page calls it the drop roll.

The answer is a ceiling, not what a buyer pays. When lots of the same item are already for sale, it often sells for much less, so check Cozy's listings with the same stats too.

Fair play

One post every 15 seconds. Your Discord name shows on everything you post. False posts get hidden and the account blocked.
